# Supply-Chain-And-Logistics-Dashboard
Project Overview
An interactive Supply Chain & Logistics Intelligence Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and Slicers to analyze revenue, product performance, logistics times, and supplier efficiency.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Total Revenue: Total sales generated across products (~577,604.82).

Units Sold: Total volume of items sold (45,952 units).

Defect Rate: Total defect rate tracked across manufacturing batches.

Manufacturing Time: Average time required for product manufacturing (15 days).

Dashboard Features & Visual Insights

Transportation & Logistics Breakdown: Horizontal bar chart comparing average shipping times vs. average shipping costs across transportation modes (Sea, Road, Rail, Air).

Supplier Performance Analysis: Line chart evaluating defect rates and average manufacturing costs across key suppliers (Supplier 1 to Supplier 5).

Product Sales Volume: Pie chart visualization breaking down the proportion of products sold by product category.

Interactive Slicers: Dynamic filters for Location (Bangalore, Chennai, Delhi, Kolkata, Mumbai), Product Type (Cosmetics, Haircare, Skincare), and Transportation Modes.
